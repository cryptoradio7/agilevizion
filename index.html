Governance

Risk register + Policies & standards + Cyber business cases

The foundation: which risks matter, which rules apply, what to fund. Building the risk register, drafting security policies, maintaining the Statement of Applicability. Building business cases for security investments and presenting the prioritised project roadmap to the executive committee for budget approval.

Operational

Security in projects + Architecture board

Embedding security into every project from day one: data classification, access control, authentication design, compliance checklist. Sitting on the architecture board to review and challenge technical choices before they go live.

Technical

Vulnerability management + Incident response

Overseeing vulnerability scans, prioritising by real business impact, driving remediation within deadlines. Running the incident response process: detect, contain, eradicate, recover, learn. Training teams so everyone knows who to call.

Resilience

Business continuity

Business Impact Analysis with the business, BCP/DRP plans, crisis simulation exercises. Making sure the organisation can survive a major incident, and has tested it, not just documented it.

Reporting

Dashboards & KPIs

Translating technical reality into metrics that Management can act on. Compliance coverage, mean time to remediate, incident trends, training completion. Proving that security works, and getting the budget to keep it running.

In practice

Problems I've actually fixed.

Anonymised situations from real engagements.

Governance
Board reporting was all green. Then an incident happened.

Reporting measured activity (number of scans launched), not effectiveness (vulnerabilities remediated on time). Rebuilt the dashboard around outcome KPIs: compliance coverage, MTTR, open critical findings. Management finally had a real picture.

Governance
ISO 27001 audit: no evidence of management involvement, risk register outdated, DdA exclusions without justification.

Restructured the risk review process with formal management sign-off, quarterly risk register updates, and documented justification for every DdA exclusion. Built the evidence trail the auditor needed.

Operational
Major platform migration: security brought in two weeks before go-live.

Introduced security-by-design checkpoints: data classification at project kickoff, access model in design phase, compliance checklist before staging. Security became a gate, not an afterthought.

Technical
M365 migration: MFA deployed, but legacy protocols (SMTP basic, POP3) left wide open in Entra ID.

Ran a CIS Benchmark assessment, blocked legacy authentication, enforced conditional access policies, removed orphan accounts. MFA actually meant something after that.

Technical
Ransomware hit a law firm. First reflex was to restore from backup. Nobody knew who to call.

Built an incident response playbook: who does what in the first hour, escalation chain, containment steps, communication template. Ran tabletop exercises so the team would react on muscle memory, not panic.

Resilience
Business continuity plan existed on paper. Never tested, staff unaware.

Ran a tabletop crisis simulation with the executive committee. Identified gaps in communication chains, recovery priorities and vendor dependencies. Updated the BCP and scheduled quarterly exercises.

Approach

The first 6 months.

A structured ramp-up, from assessment to running security function.

Month 1-2

Understand & assess

Stakeholder interviews, maturity assessment, asset inventory, quick wins (MFA, passwords, awareness). Deliver: maturity report + risk overview.

Month 3-4

Build foundations

Update security policy, set up governance committee, deploy priority standards, structure documentation framework, start risk register.

Month 5-6

Deliver & hand over

3-year security roadmap, KPI dashboard, complete handover dossier: current state, risks, stakeholder map, documentation, action plan, recommendations for the next 12 months.

Measurable progress.

Most organisations start at maturity level 1-2: informal processes, no documentation, reactive posture. After 6 months, the goal is to reach level 3 on critical domains: defined, documented, applied.

Level 1 : Nothing in place
Level 2 : Informal, ad hoc
Level 3 : Defined & applied
Level 4 : Measured & managed

After 6 months you have: a maturity assessment, governance framework, first standards deployed, quick wins in place, a security roadmap, and a dossier ready for your permanent CISO or for continued engagement.
